Logo note: the Turn2Opinion.com logo (public/brand/turn2opinion-logo.webp/png, made from the logo you supplied; a 1000 px copy is included) is the brand's own. The circular-arrows "2" mark (globe core, sign button, network node, envelope seal) is drawn as SVG in src/components/T2OMark.jsx — decorative there, because the logo itself is labelled. The globe's meridians, nodes, orbits, arcs and pings, the sparklines, gauges, the transmit beam and the envelope are CSS/SVG decoration inside elements marked `aria-hidden`; the globe as a whole is one labelled graphic that announces review progress.
